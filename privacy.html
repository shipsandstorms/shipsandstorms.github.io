Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy covers the website shipsandstorms.github.io and the "Ships and Storms uploader", an internal tool used only by the owner of the Ships and Storms YouTube channel.

The uploader tool

The uploader uses Google sign-in (OAuth) solely to let the channel owner upload videos, thumbnails and video details to the owner's own YouTube channel, and to generate images for those videos with Google Cloud services. It is not offered to the public. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are never shared, sold or transferred to third parties. No data about other Google users is collected, stored or processed.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect personal data. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to serve the pages, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

YouTube

Our videos are published on YouTube, which is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
